Services

Solar, power and electrical installation — end to end

Nine services, one team. Filter by category or open a service below for what's included.

01

Solar PV systems

Rooftop and ground-mount arrays sized to your load

Monocrystalline panel systems designed around your household or business electricity demand — covering lighting, appliances, battery charging and cooling.

Typical residential system: 3–10kW, installed within 3–5 working days.
  • Site load assessment
  • Panel & mounting supply
  • Inverter & wiring installation
  • Commissioning & handover
02

Grid-tie systems

Feed excess power back to the grid and lower your bill

Grid-tied inverters synchronise your solar generation with the utility supply, exporting surplus power and reducing net billed consumption.

Best suited to sites with consistent daytime power draw.
  • Utility-compliant inverter setup
  • Net-metering paperwork support
  • Safety isolation & protection
  • System monitoring setup
03

Power backup systems

Keep essential circuits running through an outage

Battery and inverter systems that automatically take over critical loads — lighting, routers, security systems, refrigeration — the moment grid power drops.

Typical backup runtime: 4–12 hours, depending on battery bank size.
  • Load prioritisation & wiring
  • Battery bank sizing
  • Automatic transfer switch
  • Runtime testing
04

Solar pumping systems

Off-grid water pumping for farms and homes

Solar-powered submersible and surface pumps that move water for irrigation, livestock and domestic use without diesel or a grid connection.

Running cost after installation is close to zero — no fuel, minimal maintenance.
  • Borehole / water source assessment
  • Pump & panel sizing
  • Controller installation
  • Flow testing
05

Solar water heating

Cut water-heating costs by up to 70%

Flat-plate and vacuum-tube solar water heaters sized to household or institutional hot-water demand, with electric backup for cloudy days.

Popular with hotels, hospitals and schools with high daily hot-water use.
  • Hot-water demand assessment
  • Collector & tank installation
  • Electric backup element wiring
  • Insulation & piping
06

General electrical wiring

Wiring and rewiring for any building type

New wiring, rewiring and compliance upgrades for offices, factories, schools, hospitals and hotels, carried out to code and certified on completion.

We issue a certificate of compliance on every completed wiring job.
  • Load & circuit design
  • Wiring / rewiring
  • Distribution board upgrades
  • Compliance certification
07

Electric fencing

Perimeter security for homes, farms and industrial sites

Energiser-driven perimeter fencing that deters intrusion while meeting relevant safety standards for occupied and livestock boundaries.

Can be paired with CCTV for layered perimeter security.
  • Perimeter survey
  • Energiser & wire installation
  • Warning signage
  • Earthing & safety testing
08

CCTV installation

Continuous monitoring for homes and businesses

Camera systems sized to your premises, with remote viewing so you can check in from anywhere, day or night.

Remote viewing is set up on your phone before we leave site.
  • Site coverage planning
  • Camera & DVR/NVR installation
  • Remote-access app setup
  • Footage storage configuration
