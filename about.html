About us

Powering the region with clean, reliable energy

BrightCurrent Solutions designs, supplies and installs solar, power backup and electrical systems for homes, farms and businesses — engineered properly, from survey to switch-on.

Our story

Research before installation

BrightCurrent Solutions was founded after a multi-year study into regional solar demand, which found that the vast majority of households and businesses in our market were still unsure how solar power actually works, or who to trust to install it properly. We set out to close that gap — combining certified engineering with straightforward pricing and dependable after-sales support.

Preceded by study of regional solar demand · target market where roughly 90% of respondents were unsure how solar power works.
